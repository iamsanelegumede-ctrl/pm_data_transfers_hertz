1.1 - How long will it take to download a 40GB movie with a consistent 2000Mbps internet speed? (2 marks)
1GB = 1024MB
40GB = 40960MB
1MB = 8Mb
40960MB = 327680Mb
so 327680 / 2000 = 163.84 seconds

1.2 - If a user downloads that same 40GB movie in two hours at a consistent internet speed, what is their internet speed (In MB)? (2 marks)
2hours = 7200 seconds
40GB = 40960 MB
so 40960 / 7200 = 5.69MB per second

1.3 - How many times faster is 440 Mbps than 128Kbps? (2 marks)
440Mbps = 440000Kbps
so 440000 / 128 = 3437.5 times faster

1.4 - What is the cycle time of a bus operating at 120MHz? (2 marks)
20MHz = 120 000 000Hz (120 * 10^6)
cycle time = 1 / 120 * 10^6
= 0.000 000 00833
= 8.33 nanoseconds
